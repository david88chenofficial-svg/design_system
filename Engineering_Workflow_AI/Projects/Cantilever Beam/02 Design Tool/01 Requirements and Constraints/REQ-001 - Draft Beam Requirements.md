# Draft Beam Requirements

These values are illustrative inputs for testing the workflow, not approved engineering requirements.

| Requirement | Draft value | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| Span | 0.300 m | proposed |
| First bending frequency | ≥ 60 Hz | proposed |
| Tip load | 10 N | proposed |
| Tip deflection under load | ≤ 1.0 mm | proposed |
| Strength factor | ≥ 2 against declared allowable | definition TBD |
| Objective | minimise beam mass | proposed |

## Open definitions

- Service environment, fatigue life, load spectrum, fatigue metric, and failure definition. These may not become design constraints until [[ANA-03 - Fatigue Assessment Qualification]] is qualified.
- Material allowables and their source.
- Minimum manufacturable dimensions.
- Treatment of clamp compliance and dimensional tolerance.
- Approval authority.
