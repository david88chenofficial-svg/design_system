# Cantilever Impact Test

## Claim to test

The selected beam theory and clamp representation predict the first natural frequency within the declared domain and error threshold.

## Proposed method

- Use impact excitation and measure a frequency-response function.
- Document the specimen geometry, mass, material-property source, grip configuration, sensors, sampling, windows and repeatability.
- Use at least three specimens or configurations spanning the proposed slenderness range.
- Keep model-parameter identification cases separate from final validation cases.

ISO 7626-5:2019 is a relevant standard for impact-excitation mobility measurements, and ISO 21289:2008 identifies parameters for vibration-data acquisition and reporting. The complete standards are not included in this vault and must be consulted before executing a controlled test.

## Acceptance

- Proposed frequency error: ≤ 5% for every accepted validation case.

- Static-response validation: separate plan required.
