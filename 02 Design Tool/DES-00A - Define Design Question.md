# DES-00A - Define Design Question

## Fundamental question

Given the operating conditions, constraints and an approved simulation-tool release, what seal geometry is the best supported design for the stated objectives?

## Required definition

- operating envelope and boundary conditions;
- objectives and their priorities, such as leakage and force balance;
- mandatory limits and tolerances;
- permitted design variables;
- required evidence and approval criteria;
- simulation-tool release authorised for evaluation.

The design question must be specific enough that candidate designs can be compared consistently. Continue with [[DES-01 - Requirements and Constraints]].
