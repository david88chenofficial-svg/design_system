# Why This Clamp Representation?

## Candidates

- Ideal fixed boundary: simplest and suitable for analytical verification, but may overstate stiffness.
- Rotational spring: compact representation of grip compliance, but introduces an identified parameter.
- Explicit grip model: potentially more physical, but adds geometry, contact and mesh decisions.

## Selection basis

Use repeated physical tests and a documented grip configuration. Select a representation only if it meets the frequency-error threshold without fitting and validating on the same specimens.

## Evidence

[[VAL-001 - Cantilever Impact Test]] must record the grip arrangement and repeatability. No supporting dataset exists yet.

## Conclusion

Open. The ideal fixed boundary is a verification reference, not an approved physical model.
