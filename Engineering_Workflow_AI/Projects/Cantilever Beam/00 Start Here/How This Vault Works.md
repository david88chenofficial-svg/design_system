# How This Vault Works

Read the main stream from left to right in [[Engineering Workflow.canvas]]. Follow branches downward only when the reason or evidence is needed.

The recursive pattern is:

`stage → decision → reason → evidence or code`

`open` and `TBD` are unknowns, not facts. A passing software test is verification of implementation; it is not physical validation. A design candidate cannot become approved unless it traces to adopted requirements, a qualified analysis release, calculation results and explicit engineering review.
