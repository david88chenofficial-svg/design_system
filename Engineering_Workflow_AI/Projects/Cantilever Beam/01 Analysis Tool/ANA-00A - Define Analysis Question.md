# Define Analysis Question

## Physical system

A uniform, straight, rectangular cantilever beam attached to a nominally rigid support and vibrating in small transverse motion.

## Initial questions

1. What is the first bending natural frequency?
2. What are the static tip deflection and root bending stress under a transverse tip load?

## Intended study envelope

- Length: 0.2–0.5 m.
- Rectangular section with proposed slenderness `L/h ≥ 10`.
- Homogeneous, isotropic, linear-elastic material.
- Small motion and small strain.
- Bare beam first; tip mass and support compliance are later capabilities.

This is a proposed study envelope, not a validated domain.

## Required fidelity

- First natural frequency: proposed validation error ≤ 5% against physical measurements.
- Static response: acceptance threshold TBD.
- Measurement and material-property uncertainty must be reported before qualification.
