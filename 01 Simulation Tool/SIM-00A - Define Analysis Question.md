# SIM-00A - Define Analysis Question

## Fundamental question

How does the seal perform for a specified geometry and set of operating conditions, with sufficient accuracy and traceability to support engineering design?

## Initial required outputs

- pressure distribution through the seal and balancing chamber;
- leakage or mass flow;
- stated accuracy, uncertainty and validity domain.

## Later required outputs

- axial forces and force response to gap or displacement;
- effects of manufacturing variation, wear, off-centre position and rotor tilt;
- transient chamber behaviour;
- vibration frequency, amplitude and relevant FSI response.
