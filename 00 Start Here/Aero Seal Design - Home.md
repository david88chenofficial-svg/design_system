## Working principle

The main stream follows the order in which the tool is developed. Each real engineering choice links to a short decision note, its reason, and the supporting evidence or code.

> [!warning]
> Empty, provisional or unvalidated records are not engineering approval. An LLM must follow links to evidence and must not invent missing values.
