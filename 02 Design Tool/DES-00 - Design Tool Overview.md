# DES-00 - Design Tool Overview

## Contract

Inputs: design requirements, constraints, variables/rules and an approved simulation-tool release.

Outputs: traceable candidate geometries, analysis results, design claims, iteration reasons and an approved design decision.

## Stages

- [[DES-00A - Define Design Question]]
- [[DES-01 - Requirements and Constraints]]
- [[DES-02 - Variables and Design Rules]]
- [[DES-03 - LLM Design Iterations]]
- [[DES-04 - Candidate Designs]]
- [[DES-05 - Design Evidence and Approval]]

The stages follow [[Stage Development Cycle]]. The LLM may propose and explain iterations, but it may not invent requirements, reasons or evidence, or silently change approved rules.
