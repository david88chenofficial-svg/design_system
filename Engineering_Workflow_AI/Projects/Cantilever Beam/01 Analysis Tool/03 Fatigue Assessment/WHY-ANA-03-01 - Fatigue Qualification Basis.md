# Fatigue Qualification Basis

## Why a separate qualification is required

A verified implementation of beam equations does not establish the physical validity of stress cycles, fatigue material data, load-spectrum assumptions, or life predictions. Baseline frequency validation alone also does not demonstrate that the static or cyclic stress response used for fatigue is accurate.

## Selection basis

A proposed fatigue method must be assessed against a common, declared set of cases using:

- a defined fatigue quantity and failure criterion;
- traceable loading histories or spectra and mean-stress treatment;
- material and condition data applicable to the assessed component;
- verified calculation implementation and independently validated physical response and fatigue evidence; and
- stated uncertainty, applicability limits, and acceptance criterion.
